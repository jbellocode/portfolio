BIM-SPEED – Overheating under Climate Change, Gdynia

BIM‑SPEED

Horizon 2020 · TU Berlin · 2024

Within the BIM-SPEED project on BIM for deep renovation, I simulated overheating in a 1961 duplex in Gdynia, Poland: 83 EnergyPlus runs comparing the building as built with an upgraded envelope, under historical weather and RCP 8.5 projections, assessed with the ASHRAE 55 adaptive comfort model.

Upgrades cut overheating sharply but still miss the 200-hour limit in future climates, so shading and cooling come next.
