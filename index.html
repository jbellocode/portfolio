Research projects

Horizon Europe · Master's thesis · 2025–26 · with Mainflux Labs

A calibrated digital twin of the 3L office building

REINCARNATE

Problem

Office zones were overheating, and the building model and measured data were never connected.

Method

Python BIM-to-BEM from IFC to EnergyPlus; calibration on hourly indoor temperature and weather data; control logic for TABS tested in simulation.

Result

CV(RMSE) 9.65%, RMSE 2.34 °C. TABS control limits peaks to about 26 °C, with the comfort–energy trade-off quantified.

Horizon 2020 · TU Berlin · 2024

Will a 1961 duplex in Gdynia overheat as the climate warms?

BIM‑SPEED

Problem

Existing homes in cool climates are not designed for future heatwaves. How much will they overheat, and do envelope upgrades help?

Method

83 EnergyPlus runs, as built vs. upgraded envelope, under 2017–21 weather and RCP 8.5 projections for 2040 and 2070; ASHRAE 55 adaptive comfort.

Result

Upgrades cut overheating from 2,700 to 638 hours by 2061–80, but not below the 200-hour limit. Shading and cooling are needed next.

  • ASHRAE 55
  • RCP 8.5
