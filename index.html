Digital Twins · REINCARNATE & Mainflux Labs · 2025–26

Calibrated digital twin of the 3L office building

Modelled the building in EnergyPlus and combined it with measured indoor conditions and weather data to investigate overheating and operational behaviour.

  • Python BIM-to-BEM workflow: IFC geometry → EnergyPlus IDF, linked to operational data
  • Calibrated against hourly measured temperatures: CV(RMSE) 9.65%, RMSE 2.34 °C
  • Simulation-tested Python control logic for TABS, holding peak indoor temperatures to ≈26 °C while quantifying the comfort–energy trade-off

EnergyPlus · Python · IfcOpenShell · IFC
